# Cohnsoft Lookout artwork

Original Cohnsoft product artwork, also used by the live Cohnsoft catalogue. Editable SVG and Cohnsoft Sprite documents are included. The PNG is rasterized from the original SVG at 1024px and exported through the pinned Cohnsoft Sprite document/session/export engine in cohnsoftassets/tools/sprite at 752e894f90f1e58e62ae466352cb03c8bfd7d703. ICNS and WebP are derived from that PNG. Canonical company mark and Cohn Sans are copied unchanged. No third-party artwork.
